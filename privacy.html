Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Files stay on your device

PDF Metadata Remover and its EXIF Remover process files locally in your web browser. Your selected PDFs and photos are not uploaded to our servers, stored by us, or shared with third parties.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not collect the contents or metadata of your PDFs or photos.
  • We do not store original or cleaned files.

Analytics and hosting

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless analytics service, to measure aggregate page views and website performance. It does not inspect your files or track you across websites. Our hosting provider may also process basic technical request data needed to deliver the website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its processing model changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
